01 — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

How Ponsly handles information when you use the app.

Ponsly is a Web3 application operating on Arc Mainnet. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you interact with Ponsly, and how that information may be used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Ponsly, we may process information such as:

  • Wallet address — the public address of the wallet you connect.
  • Blockchain transaction information — data related to transactions you submit through Ponsly.
  • Information you voluntarily provide — such as token details you enter when launching.
  • Information related to your use of the website — such as pages viewed and general interaction with the interface.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

We do not collect personal information beyond what is described in this Policy or reasonably necessary to operate Ponsly.

2. Wallet Information

Connecting a wallet to Ponsly may make your wallet's public blockchain address visible to Ponsly. Wallet addresses are public blockchain information, and transactions submitted through your wallet are publicly recorded on-chain regardless of your use of Ponsly.

3. Social Accounts

Ponsly may offer optional social-account integrations. Where such integrations exist, information from them is processed only if and when you choose to connect or use that integration. Connecting a social account is optional and is not required to use Ponsly's core features.

4. Blockchain Data

Transactions submitted through Ponsly are recorded on a public blockchain and may be permanently visible to anyone. Ponsly does not control the underlying blockchain and cannot alter, hide, or remove data once it has been recorded on-chain.

5. How Information Is Used

Information we process may be used to:

  • Operate Ponsly
  • Provide the features you request
  • Process transactions
  • Maintain the security of the service
  • Prevent abuse and fraudulent activity
  • Improve Ponsly over time
  • Communicate important service information

6. Third-Party Services

Ponsly may interact with third-party infrastructure required to operate the application, including blockchain networks, APIs, wallet providers, exchanges, and analytics or infrastructure providers. These third parties operate under their own terms and privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by Ponsly. However, no method of transmission, storage, or blockchain interaction can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

How long information is retained depends on the type of information and applicable operational or legal requirements. Blockchain records cannot be deleted or modified by Ponsly once they have been recorded on-chain.

9. User Rights

You may contact us regarding applicable privacy-related requests. We will respond as appropriate based on the nature of the request and applicable requirements. This Policy does not make jurisdiction-specific legal guarantees.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page, and continued use of Ponsly after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. Contact

Contact information will be provided through Ponsly's official communication channels.

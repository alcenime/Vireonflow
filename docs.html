01 — Overview

Overview

Launch. Trade. Earn.

Vireon is a creator-focused token launchpad built on Robinhood Chain.

Vireon gives creators a dedicated launch experience while connecting launched tokens to the broader Pons trading ecosystem. Instead of building a separate exchange, Vireon focuses on making the launch experience simple, transparent, and creator-first.

What is Vireon?

Vireon provides a simple launch experience for creators who want to launch tokens on Robinhood Chain. Tokens launched through Vireon are surfaced through Vireon and the Pons trading ecosystem.

04 — Earning

Staking

Stake to earn a share of each Epoch's reward pool.

Staking rewards are distributed based on the points earned by each staker during the Epoch. The more tokens a user stakes, and the longer they remain staked during the Epoch, the more points they accumulate.

3 days

Based
Proportional
Reward share

Points formula

Each staker's points are calculated from their staked amount and how many hours they remained staked during the Epoch:

Pointsi = (StakedAmounti / 1,000) × HoursStakedi

Total points across all stakers in the Epoch:

TotalPoints = Σ Pointsi

Reward formula

Each staker's share of the Epoch reward pool is proportional to their share of total points:

Rewardi = (Pointsi / TotalPoints) × EpochRewardPool

Example

A wallet staking 10,000 tokens for 72 hours during the Epoch:

Points = (10,000 / 1,000) × 72 = 720

The more tokens a user stakes and the longer they remain staked during the Epoch, the more points they accumulate. Rewards are proportional to the user's share of the total Epoch points.

06 — Economics

Tokenomics

How Vireon's fee revenue is allocated.

Vireon allocates its fee revenue across rewards, token buyback and burn, and the team.

Rewards — 70%

The Rewards allocation supports both the Staking and Fee Claim programs.

Buyback & Burn — 10%

Used to buy back the Vireon token and burn the purchased tokens.

Team — 20%

Allocated to the Vireon team.

The launch fee paid when launching a token is separate from tokenomics. Tokenomics describes how the applicable fee revenue received by Vireon is allocated. Vireon does not keep a trading fee.

07 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to connect X to launch a token?
No. Connecting X is not required to launch a token.
How long is a staking Epoch?
Each staking Epoch lasts 3 days.
How are staking rewards calculated?
Rewards are calculated proportionally based on each wallet's staking points during the Epoch.
What is the staking points formula?
Points = (Staked Amount / 1,000) × Hours Staked.
How much is the Fee Claim reward?
Eligible wallets can claim 0.0005 ETH, subject to the applicable campaign requirements.
How is Vireon's fee revenue allocated?
70% goes to Rewards, 10% to Buyback & Burn, and 20% to the Team.
How many Fee Claims are available?
The current campaign is limited to 100 FCFS claims per day, resetting at 00:00 UTC.
Can one wallet claim multiple Fee Claims?
No. One wallet can make one Fee Claim under the campaign.
Will my Vireon token appear on Pons?
Tokens launched through Vireon are connected to the Pons trading ecosystem, subject to the applicable launch and trading infrastructure.
